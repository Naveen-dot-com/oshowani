Privacy Policy

Short version: Oshowani does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your conversations stay on your device. We do not track you.

Who We Are

Oshowani is a spiritual chat application developed by Naveen Dubey, an independent developer based in Varanasi, India.

Questions? Contact us at n.k.dubey1997@gmail.com

What Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. No account, no sign-in, no personal information required.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your location or IP address
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Payment information of any kind

Conversation Data

Your messages are sent to Google Gemini API over encrypted HTTPS to generate responses. We do not store your conversations on any server we control.

Your chat history is saved only on your own device in browser local storage. Clearing app data permanently deletes it.

What Is Stored Locally

  • Your chat history and past conversations
  • Your language preference
  • Your theme preference (light / dark)

This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Google Gemini API — used to generate AI responses. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy. No analytics, advertising networks, or trackers are used.

Children's Privacy

Oshowani is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

Since we store no personal data server-side, you can delete all your data at any time by clearing your browser or app storage.
